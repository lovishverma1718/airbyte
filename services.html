Furnaces

Heating

A dependable furnace is the heart of a comfortable winter. We install high-efficiency gas furnaces sized to your home, diagnose and repair problems quickly, and keep existing systems running safely with annual maintenance.

Signs it’s time: uneven heat, short-cycling, rising gas bills, strange noises or a unit over 15 years old.

What we handle

  • New high-efficiency furnace installation
  • Furnace replacement & upgrades
  • Diagnostics & repairs
  • Annual tune-ups & safety checks
  • Filter & thermostat upgrades

02 / 09

Heat Pumps

HeatingCooling

Heat pumps move heat instead of making it, so they warm your home in winter and cool it in summer with remarkable efficiency. The Lower Mainland’s mild climate makes them one of the smartest upgrades you can make.

Good to know: a heat pump can be paired with your existing gas furnace as a hybrid system for the coldest days.

What we handle

  • Ducted central heat pumps
  • Ductless mini-splits for any room
  • Hybrid / dual-fuel systems
  • Sizing, design & load calculations
  • Repairs & seasonal maintenance
Ask about heat pumps

Cooling

Hi-Velocity Systems

HeatingCooling

Hi-Velocity systems use flexible, small-diameter ducts that fit inside existing walls and ceilings. They’re the go-to choice for older and character homes, renovations and spaces where full-size ductwork simply won’t fit.

Why it works: minimal demolition, discreet outlets and even temperatures from room to room.

What we handle

  • Hi-Velocity heating & cooling installs
  • Retrofits for older & character homes
  • Small-duct system design
  • Service & repairs

Gas Fitting

Gas & water

Gas work is not a DIY job. Our licensed gas fitting covers new lines and connections for appliances indoors and out, all installed safely, pressure-tested and done to code.

Smell gas? Leave the building right away and call FortisBC’s emergency line or 911 from outside.

What we handle

  • Gas ranges & cooktops
  • BBQ & patio heater lines
  • Gas dryers & appliances
  • New gas lines for renovations
  • Leak checks & safety inspections

Hot Water Tanks

Gas & water

No hot water is never convenient. We repair and replace hot water tanks quickly, and help you decide whether a conventional tank or an on-demand tankless heater is the better fit for your household.

Watch for: rusty water, pooling around the base, popping sounds or a tank over 10 years old.

What we handle

  • Hot water tank replacement
  • Tankless water heater installs
  • Repairs & diagnostics
  • Expansion tanks & venting

08 / 09

HRV Systems

Air quality

Modern homes are built tight, which is great for efficiency but not for air quality. A heat recovery ventilator constantly exchanges stale indoor air for fresh, filtered outdoor air while recovering most of the heat.

Helps with: window condensation, lingering odours, stuffiness and excess humidity.

What we handle

  • HRV installation & replacement
  • Airflow balancing
  • Filter & core cleaning
  • Controls & troubleshooting
Improve your air
09 / 09

Ventilation

Air quality

Good airflow is what makes heating and cooling feel even. We design and install ductwork, fix weak or noisy airflow, and set up kitchen and bathroom exhaust so moisture and odours leave your home where they should.

Common fixes: rooms that never warm up, whistling vents and bathrooms that stay steamy.

What we handle

  • Ductwork design & installation
  • Duct modifications for renovations
  • Bathroom & kitchen exhaust
  • Airflow diagnostics & balancing
Book ventilation work
